# Benefit Infographic Prompts

## Core Benefit Infographic

```text
Create a premium Amazon product infographic for [product name], focused on [benefit]. The image should answer the shopper concern: [user concern]. Use a clean layout with one headline area, accurate product placement, restrained callouts, and visual proof such as [proof element]. Keep the product as the visual anchor, preserve accurate structure, color, material, scale, and logo placement, and use realistic studio lighting with soft shadows. The design should feel refined and easy to scan, avoiding cluttered icon walls, excessive arrows, cheap sales badges, fake glow effects, exaggerated claims, crowded copy, and any invented product functions.
```

## Pain Point Proof Infographic

```text
Create a conversion-focused Amazon PDP image for [product name] that turns the user pain point [pain point] into a clear visual proof. Show [product feature] solving the problem through [scene, comparison, close-up, or before/after proof]. Use a premium, product-first design with controlled lighting, realistic details, clear headline hierarchy, and enough negative space for readability. Keep all product details accurate and believable. The image should look like a high-quality brand listing, not a generic AI ad or low-end marketplace graphic.
```
