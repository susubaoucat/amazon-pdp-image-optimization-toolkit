# Lifestyle Scene Prompts

## Real Usage Scene

```text
Create a realistic premium lifestyle scene for [product name] in [target market] context, showing [target user] using the product to solve [user concern]. The environment should feel believable, refined, and relevant to the marketplace, such as [home type, room, surface, or situation]. Keep the product accurate in shape, color, material, and scale, and make it the visual focus without making the image feel staged or cluttered. Use soft natural light, restrained colors, realistic shadows, and a clean composition suitable for Amazon PDP gallery use. Avoid fake features, exaggerated motion, unrealistic room scale, excessive props, over-designed typography, and generic stock-photo styling.
```

## Storage or Usability Scene

```text
Create a premium Amazon PDP lifestyle image for [product name], focused on [storage, setup, maintenance, or daily handling benefit]. Show the product in a realistic [target market] home environment, with clear spatial proof such as [under sofa, closet corner, kitchen cabinet, apartment entryway, utility room]. Use clean composition, believable scale, soft lighting, and restrained visual hierarchy. Preserve the product's real structure and material, and make the image feel practical, refined, and trustworthy rather than decorative or overly promotional.
```
