# A+ Banner Prompts

## Premium A+ Hero Banner

```text
Create a premium Amazon A+ hero banner for [product name], positioning it as [positioning]. The banner should communicate [main value proposition] through a refined full-width composition with the product as the visual anchor, generous negative space, controlled studio or lifestyle lighting, realistic shadows, and restrained color palette. Include room for a short headline and supporting line without overcrowding. The image should feel high-end, credible, and product-specific, avoiding generic AI luxury styling, excessive glow, fake HUD graphics, cluttered badges, unrealistic claims, and distorted product details.
```

## Technology Proof Banner

```text
Create a clean Amazon A+ technology proof banner for [product name], explaining [technology or feature] in a visually understandable way. Use a realistic product close-up or cutaway-inspired layout only if it does not invent internal parts. Combine product detail, simple diagram logic, restrained labels, and premium lighting. The final image should make the feature feel credible and easy to understand, avoiding fake sci-fi interfaces, complex technical clutter, exaggerated claims, and any inaccurate product structure.
```
