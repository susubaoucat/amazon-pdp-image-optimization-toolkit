# Product Hero Image Prompts

These prompts are starting points. Replace bracketed fields with product-specific information.

## Main Image Prompt

```text
Create a clean Amazon main product image for [product name], showing only the actual product and included accessories on a pure white background. The product should be centered, sharply lit, in focus, and occupy most of the frame while preserving accurate shape, color, material, proportions, logo placement, and included parts. Use professional studio lighting, realistic shadows, and a product-first composition suitable for Amazon search and PDP use. Do not add lifestyle props, decorative text, badges, watermarks, fake features, extra accessories, unrealistic glow effects, or altered product structure.
```

## Premium Gallery Hero Prompt

```text
Create a premium Amazon PDP gallery hero image for [product name], focused on [core benefit]. Use a refined product-first composition with generous negative space, soft controlled studio lighting, realistic shadows, restrained neutral colors, and one clear visual message. Show the product accurately in [usage or display angle], with believable scale and material detail. The image should feel high-end, clean, and conversion-focused, avoiding crowded marketplace collage design, excessive arrows, fake technology effects, over-saturated backgrounds, decorative typography, and any distortion of the product.
```
