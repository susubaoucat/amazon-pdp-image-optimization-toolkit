## Premium Taste Rules

- Keep each module focused on one message.
- Use consistent spacing, lighting, and color logic across modules.
- Avoid visual overload and repeated icon walls.
- Keep product renderings accurate and consistent.
- Use realistic scenes that match the target market.
- Make technical diagrams feel clear and restrained.
