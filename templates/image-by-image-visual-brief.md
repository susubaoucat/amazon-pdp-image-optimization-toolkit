## QA Checklist

- Product shape is accurate.
- Product color and material are accurate.
- Product scale is believable.
- The image communicates one main message.
- The headline is short and readable.
- The layout feels premium, not crowded.
- The visual proof supports the claim.
- No fake features are shown.
- The image fits Amazon PDP context.
