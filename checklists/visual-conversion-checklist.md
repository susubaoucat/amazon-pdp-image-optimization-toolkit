# Visual Conversion Checklist

Use this to judge whether an image helps a shopper decide.

## Message Clarity

- One image communicates one main message.
- Headline is short and benefit-led.
- Supporting text proves the headline instead of repeating it.
- Visual proof appears before decorative detail.
- The shopper can understand the image without reading long copy.

## Premium Taste

- Composition has enough negative space.
- Product is the visual anchor.
- Color palette is restrained and intentional.
- Lighting feels realistic and controlled.
- Typography is simple, readable, and not decorative.
- Icons and callouts are used sparingly.
- The image does not feel like a cheap sales poster.
- The design avoids crowded collages, random gradients, excessive arrows, fake glow, and generic AI luxury styling.

## Review Objection Handling

- Own review concerns are answered visually.
- Competitor review weaknesses become clear visual opportunities.
- Claims are supported by scene, close-up, comparison, or measurable proof.
- The image reduces hesitation rather than only listing features.

## Market Fit

- Home scene, room size, props, and lifestyle context fit the target market.
- The visual tone matches local shopper expectations.
- Copy length and hierarchy fit the market's reading behavior.
- The image avoids stereotypes or unrealistic local cues.
