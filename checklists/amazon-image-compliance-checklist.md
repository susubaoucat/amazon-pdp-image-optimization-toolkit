# Amazon Image Compliance Checklist

Use this as a practical pre-publish review. Always verify against current Amazon rules for the target category and marketplace.

## Main Image

- Product is shown on a pure white background.
- Product is the actual item being sold.
- Product fills the frame clearly and is not too small.
- Image is sharp, well-lit, and not pixelated.
- No text, icons, badges, decorative graphics, or watermarks.
- No props or accessories that are not included.
- Product shape, color, and material are accurate.
- Image is large enough for zoom.

## Gallery Images

- Each image has one clear message.
- Claims are visually supported and not exaggerated.
- Lifestyle scenes match the product's real use.
- Comparison visuals are fair and not misleading.
- Text is readable at PDP gallery size.
- Product is not distorted by AI generation.
- Props do not imply unsupported features or accessories.
- Visual style is consistent across the image set.

## A+ Images

- A+ modules follow a clear narrative order.
- Each module has a defined role in the purchase journey.
- Technical visuals do not invent internal structures.
- Brand tone, lighting, and color are consistent.
- Copy is concise and legible.
- Visual proof supports the product claim.
