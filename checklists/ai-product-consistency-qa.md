# AI Product Consistency QA

Use this after generating product images with AI.

## Product Accuracy

- Product silhouette matches the source product.
- Handle, buttons, joints, wheels, vents, ports, brush heads, caps, labels, and seams are in the correct location.
- Logo and brand marks are not misspelled, warped, duplicated, or invented.
- Product color is consistent with the source image.
- Material finish looks believable.
- Product scale is realistic compared with hand, furniture, room, or accessories.
- No extra parts or missing parts appear.
- No unsupported feature is implied.

## AI Artifact Review

- No warped edges.
- No melted product details.
- No strange reflections.
- No unreadable or fake text.
- No unrealistic lighting direction.
- No duplicate product parts.
- No impossible perspective.

## Production Readiness

- Image can be understood at Amazon gallery size.
- Main benefit is visible within three seconds.
- Product remains the hero of the image.
- Image style matches the rest of the PDP set.
- Prompt can be refined using the issue list above.
