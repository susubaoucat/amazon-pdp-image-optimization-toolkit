# Example: Cordless Vacuum PDP Image Optimization

This example shows how the toolkit turns product context, reviews, and competitor signals into image production briefs.

## Product Context

- Product: Foldable cordless vacuum cleaner
- Category: Home cleaning appliance
- Target market: Japan
- Core benefits: Foldable storage, lightweight handling, anti-tangle brush, strong suction, easy maintenance
- Optimization goal: Make the PDP feel more premium and better answer daily-use concerns in compact homes

## PDP Image Diagnosis

| Image | Diagnosis | Rewrite Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Image 1 | Main image is acceptable but product appears slightly small. | Enlarge product within frame and keep pure white background. |
| Image 2 | Feature infographic lists suction power but does not connect it to daily cleaning pain points. | Rebuild as a core benefit proof image focused on dust, pet hair, and edge cleaning. |
| Image 3 | Foldable design is shown, but storage benefit is not emotionally clear. | Show the folded product in a compact apartment storage scene. |
| Image 4 | Lifestyle image feels generic and does not match Japanese home scale. | Use a smaller, cleaner apartment scene with restrained props. |
| Image 5 | Anti-tangle feature is described with too much text. | Use a brush close-up and simple before/after hair proof. |
| Image 6 | Maintenance information is missing. | Add a clean water/dust cup removal and washing image. |

## Review Insight to Image Opportunity

| Review Signal | Image Opportunity |
| --- | --- |
| Users care about storage and daily handling. | Add a lightweight folded-storage image. |
| Competitor reviews complain about hair tangling. | Add an anti-tangle brush proof image. |
| Users want stronger cleaning confidence. | Add a suction proof infographic tied to dust, debris, and edge cleaning. |
| Users dislike complicated maintenance. | Add an easy-maintenance module with removable cup and washable parts. |

## Image-by-Image PDP Plan

### Image 1: Main Product Image

**Goal:** Make the product clear and credible in search and PDP gallery.

**User Concern:** Is this the actual product and what is included?

**Visual Concept:** Clean white-background packshot showing the vacuum and included accessories.

**Composition:** Product centered, large in frame, accessories arranged neatly, no text.

**Copy Hierarchy:** No copy.

**Product Usage:** Preserve real shape, color, brush head, handle, folding joint, and accessories.

**Prompt:**

```text
Create a clean Amazon main product image for a foldable cordless vacuum cleaner, showing only the actual vacuum and included accessories on a pure white background. The product should be centered, sharply lit, in focus, and occupy most of the frame while preserving accurate shape, color, material, brush head, handle, folding joint, logo placement, and included parts. Use professional studio lighting, realistic shadows, and a product-first composition suitable for Amazon search and PDP use. Do not add lifestyle props, decorative text, badges, watermarks, fake features, extra accessories, unrealistic glow effects, or altered product structure.
```

**QA Checklist:** Product fills the frame, no text, white background, accurate accessories, no AI distortion.

### Image 2: Core Cleaning Proof Infographic

**Goal:** Connect suction performance to real household cleaning concerns.

**User Concern:** Will it clean dust, crumbs, pet hair, and edges effectively?

**Visual Concept:** Product angled beside controlled debris zones and a simple edge-cleaning proof.

**Composition:** Product as anchor on the right, clean proof zones on the left, one short headline.

**Copy Hierarchy:** Headline: "Strong everyday cleaning." Supporting callouts: dust, crumbs, pet hair, edge cleaning.

**Product Usage:** Show realistic floor debris and avoid exaggerated mess.

**Prompt:**

```text
Create a premium Amazon product infographic for a foldable cordless vacuum cleaner, focused on strong everyday cleaning for dust, crumbs, pet hair, and edge areas. Use a clean product-first layout with one short headline area, accurate product placement, restrained callouts, and visual proof through realistic floor debris zones and an edge-cleaning scene. Keep the product as the visual anchor, preserve accurate structure, color, material, scale, and logo placement, and use realistic studio lighting with soft shadows. The design should feel refined and easy to scan, avoiding cluttered icon walls, excessive arrows, cheap sales badges, fake glow effects, exaggerated claims, crowded copy, and any invented product functions.
```

**QA Checklist:** One message, believable debris, readable hierarchy, accurate product, premium spacing.

### Image 3: Foldable Storage Scene

**Goal:** Prove compact storage for small homes.

**User Concern:** Will it be easy to store in a Japanese apartment?

**Visual Concept:** Folded vacuum stored beside a sofa or inside a narrow closet corner.

**Composition:** Small apartment scene, vacuum folded, clear spatial comparison, calm negative space.

**Copy Hierarchy:** Headline: "Fits compact spaces." Supporting text: "Fold and store after daily cleaning."

**Product Usage:** Show the real folding mechanism without changing proportions.

**Prompt:**

```text
Create a premium Amazon PDP lifestyle image for a foldable cordless vacuum cleaner, focused on compact storage for a Japanese apartment. Show the product in an accurate folded form beside a small sofa, closet corner, or apartment entryway, with clear spatial proof that it is easy to store after daily cleaning. Use clean composition, believable scale, soft natural lighting, restrained neutral colors, and a refined visual hierarchy with room for one short headline. Preserve the product's real folding structure, brush head, color, material, and proportions, and make the image feel practical, refined, and trustworthy rather than decorative, crowded, or overly promotional.
```

**QA Checklist:** Product scale is believable, Japanese home context is realistic, folding joint remains accurate.

### Image 4: Anti-Tangle Brush Proof

**Goal:** Turn competitor hair-tangle complaints into a visual advantage.

**User Concern:** Will hair get stuck in the brush?

**Visual Concept:** Brush head close-up with simple hair-management proof.

**Composition:** Large brush close-up, one clean proof area, restrained labels.

**Copy Hierarchy:** Headline: "Designed to reduce hair tangles." Supporting text: "Cleaner brush maintenance after each use."

**Product Usage:** Do not invent internal brush structure.

**Prompt:**

```text
Create a conversion-focused Amazon PDP image for a foldable cordless vacuum cleaner that turns the user pain point of hair tangling into clear visual proof. Show an accurate close-up of the brush head and a simple realistic hair-management proof, with clean labels and a premium product-first layout. Use controlled studio lighting, realistic material detail, clear headline hierarchy, and enough negative space for readability. Keep all product details accurate and believable, especially the brush shape and mechanism. The image should look like a high-quality brand listing, not a generic AI ad or low-end marketplace graphic, and should not invent internal parts or exaggerate the claim.
```

**QA Checklist:** No fake brush structure, claim is careful, close-up is sharp, text is not crowded.

### Image 5: Easy Maintenance Image

**Goal:** Reduce concerns about cleaning the device after use.

**User Concern:** Is it hard to empty, wash, or maintain?

**Visual Concept:** Dust cup removal and washable component layout.

**Composition:** Product section detail, removable cup, clean water cue, step-by-step visual logic.

**Copy Hierarchy:** Headline: "Simple daily maintenance." Supporting callouts: empty, rinse, reattach.

**Product Usage:** Only show washable parts if they are actually washable.

**Prompt:**

```text
Create a clean premium Amazon PDP image for a foldable cordless vacuum cleaner, focused on simple daily maintenance. Show the dust cup removal and washable component process with clear step-by-step visual logic: empty, rinse, and reattach. Use accurate product details, refined studio lighting, realistic shadows, restrained labels, and a simple composition that feels trustworthy and easy to understand. Keep the product shape, cup structure, color, material, and scale accurate. Do not show parts as washable unless supported by the product information, and avoid crowded instruction graphics, excessive icons, fake water effects, or low-end marketplace styling.
```

**QA Checklist:** Maintenance claim is accurate, sequence is easy to read, no unsupported washable parts.

## Premium Taste Layer

- Use quiet, clean compositions with controlled negative space.
- Keep product accurate and dominant.
- Prefer soft natural or studio lighting over high-contrast effects.
- Use restrained neutral backgrounds with one subtle product or brand accent.
- Use short copy and clear hierarchy.
- Avoid cheap sales badges, crowded collages, random gradients, excessive arrows, fake glow, and generic AI luxury styling.

## Visual QA Checklist

- Each image solves one customer question.
- Review concerns are turned into visual proof.
- Product shape, color, material, and scale remain accurate.
- The visual set feels consistent across gallery and A+.
- The Japanese market context feels believable and not stereotyped.
- The output looks premium enough for a high-quality brand listing.
